HOW TO SKATE

Master the streets, skatepark, and underground garage

🚀 JUMP & LAUNCH OFF RAMPS

Press SPACE or tap OLLIE to jump. Curved ramps launch you extra high into the sky for massive airtime!

✨ GRIND RAILS FOR POINTS

Land your skateboard onto any metal handrail to start grinding. Grinding awards continuous points and sparks! Keep your balance inside the sweet spot.

🔥 AERIAL TRICKS (TAP TO FLIP - NO HOLDING!)

Tap trick keys while airborne: Z Kickflip, X Heelflip, C 360 Pop Shuvit, V Indy Grab. You must tap cleanly—holding trick keys is disabled! Use A/D or arrow keys to rotate and do spins.

🗺️ UNLOCK MULTIPLE LEVELS

Earn 1,000 Points to unlock the Sunset Skatepark with bowls and park rails, then earn another 1,000 Points (2,000 total) to unlock the Underground Garage with neon pipes and concrete obstacles!

🚌 BUS STOPS & RIDING BACK [B]

Bus stops appear along every district. Press B or tap the BUS button anytime to board the transit bus and ride back to previous levels!

🏪 UPGRADE AT THE SKATE SHOP

Press E or tap the Shop button anytime to upgrade your Bearings for speed and Trucks for control!
