📜 HOW TO PLAY "POTATO WARS"

🥔 The Stick Hero & The Alien Spud Boss

You play as the Potato Stickman with 5 hearts and protective potato armor. At the top of the arena, a Giant Alien Potato with wiggly antennae moves back and forth on the top platform and shoots fiery fireballs at you!

✨ Moving Platforms & Phasing Coins

All platforms are constantly moving! Collect regular floating coins (+5 coins & points each). Watch out for the Special Phase Coins: they phase directly through platforms and disappear/reappear every 2 seconds — catch them when solid for +10 coins & points! Advancing to the next wave awards +500 bonus coins and restores your health to 5 hearts!

💥 Potato Slams & Armor Upgrades

Press Spacebar or tap POTATO SLAM to execute a mighty shockwave! Slams destroy incoming fireballs and strike the Alien Potato. Press Down Arrow / S to drop down one platform at a time. When out of slams, armor, or hearts, visit the Shop to spend your saved coins (Slams = 100 coins, Armor = 150 coins, Heals = 50 coins). The Giant Alien Potato shoots fireballs (+1 more fireball every wave)!
